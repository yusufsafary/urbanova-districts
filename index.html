What decides a district verdict?

At seal time, verdict follows unique builders of that district: under 25 unique wallets means Vacant, 25 to 100 means Block, over 100 means Metropolis. One exception: a plot whose own on-chain seed lands at 970 or higher modulo 1000 turns Nova, whatever a crowd did. Extra plots from a same wallet do not raise a builder count.

What does sealed mean?

When a district day ends at 00:00 UTC, a district seals. Its plot count, builder count, and every verdict are final forever: nobody can raise more plots in it or change a verdict.

How many plots can I raise?

Up to 10 per wallet per district. Plot 1 is free for Agent ID holders, plots 2 to 5 cost 0.0002 BNB each, and plots 6 to 10 cost 0.0004 BNB each. Tomorrow is a new district with a fresh allowance of 10.

When can I claim $URBN?

A plot claims once its district is sealed, claims are open, and a daily claim window has opened: 09:00 UTC on seal day, and any time after. Amount follows final class: 2,500 URBN for Vacant, 12,500 for Block, 60,000 for Metropolis, 250,000 for a Nova plot. Each claim carries a fee in BNB, forwarded to a project wallet in a same transaction; a Nova plot pays double a base fee. Your plot is never burned.

Do I need an Agent ID?

Yes, for every plot. Holding an Agent ID NFT from a SPACE ID registry gates all building on Urbanova, free and paid alike. A free first plot also asks a short arithmetic question, answered on chain by your agent, to keep bots out of a city.
