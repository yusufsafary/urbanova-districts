$URBN tokenomics

Fixed supply · No presale · Figures below are contract rules, not targets

Supply

$URBN (contract name Urbanova) has a fixed supply of 10,000,000,000 URBN, created once at deployment. Its contract has no mint function, so supply can never grow. No $URBN was sold in a presale, and no allocation exists for advisors, exchanges, or a public sale.

Claim pool. 2,000,000,000 URBN (20% of supply) funds holder claims. It is held by an UrbanovaClaim contract and paid out only through per-plot claims at fixed amounts below. Pool status · Funding at deployment Live pool balance is readable on a homepage claim board once contracts are live.

Claim amounts per class

Every plot of a sealed district claims $URBN once, paid by its final class. Amounts are fixed in an UrbanovaClaim contract:

Claiming never burns or moves a plot. One plot claims once, ever, paid to whoever owns it at claim time. Claims for a district open at 09:00 UTC on its seal day.

Fee mechanics

  • Claim fee: each claim call attaches a base fee in BNB, set as an immutable value in an UrbanovaClaim contract and forwarded to a project wallet inside a same transaction. A Nova plot pays exactly double a base fee. An UrbanovaClaim contract keeps no BNB balance.
  • Paid plots: plots 2 to 5 cost 0.0002 BNB each and plots 6 to 10 cost 0.0004 BNB each. Payments forward to a project wallet inside a same transaction; an UrbanovaPlots contract keeps no balance.
  • Token transfers: $URBN itself carries no transfer tax in its contract.

Pool status

No $URBN liquidity pool exists yet, so $URBN cannot be swapped today. Swap routes on this site are prefilled and honestly marked Pool · Soon. If a pool is created, it will be funded from a project wallet allocation and announced on a homepage and a swap page. See swap.

What $URBN is not

$URBN is not sold by this site, carries no promise of price, yield, or buyback, and is not a security or an investment product. Read a Disclaimer before interacting with any Urbanova contract.
