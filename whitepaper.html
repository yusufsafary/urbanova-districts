Urbanova · Whitepaper

A city in daily districts

Concept, mechanics, security, risks

1 · A city in daily districts

Urbanova Districts is a city that grows in public, one district per day, on BNB Chain. Each day opens a fresh district. Holders of a Binance Agent ID, acting through an agentic wallet, raise plots inside a running district. At 00:00 UTC a district seals forever, and a verdict fixed at seal time decides what every plot of that day is worth in $URBN claims.

A design goal is legibility: one period per day, one free plot per wallet per district, a price ladder anyone can memorize, and a verdict rule a child can check. No curves, no referrals, no rarity rolls beyond one published seed rule.

2 · Daily loop

  • Open. District 1 (Genesis) opens by hand; its window ends at a next 00:00 UTC. Every later district runs a plain UTC day, 00:00 to 24:00 UTC.
  • Build. A first plot per wallet per district is free, gated by Agent ID ownership and a short on-chain arithmetic question (salt URBANOVA-Q). Plots 2 to 5 cost 0.0002 BNB each; plots 6 to 10 cost 0.0004 BNB each. Cap: 10 plots per wallet per district.
  • Seal. At 00:00 UTC a district seals. Plot count, unique builder count, and verdict become final and can never change.
  • Claim. From 09:00 UTC on seal day, every plot of a sealed district claims $URBN once, by final class. Claims stay open afterwards.

3 · Verdict mechanics

A verdict follows unique builders: distinct wallets that raised at least one plot in a district, counted on chain as participantsOf(district). Extra plots from a wallet that already built do not raise a count.

Over 100Metropolis60,000 URBN

Nova exception. Each plot carries an on-chain seed derived at mint time. A plot with seed % 1000 of 970 or higher (about 3 in 100) is a Nova plot: it claims 250,000 URBN regardless of its district verdict, and pays double a base claim fee.

4 · Money flow

Every payment moves inside a same transaction that creates value. Paid plot BNB forwards in full to a project wallet during mintPaid; an UrbanovaPlots contract holds no balance. A claim fee forwards in full to a project wallet during claim; an UrbanovaClaim contract holds only its $URBN pool. Nothing routes through this website, and this website never takes custody of funds.

5 · Security model

  • Agent-only surface. Mint and claim calls reject contract callers (tx.origin must equal a caller), and free mints require an Agent ID plus a per-wallet arithmetic answer computed on chain.
  • Immutable economics. Prices, caps, verdict thresholds, claim amounts, and claim fees are constants or immutables in deployed contracts. An owner can only open Genesis, pause or resume claims, and set a metadata base URI.
  • Verified code. Contracts publish as Sourcify-verified source at deployment, and addresses live in one public config file agents must read.

6 · Risks

$URBN has no promised market, price, or liquidity. A pool may never open. Smart contracts can contain defects despite testing and verification. Participation is voluntary and experimental; read a Disclaimer and a Terms of Use before building.
