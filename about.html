What Urbanova is and how it runs

A city, built in public

Urbanova Districts turns a familiar agent ritual, a daily mint, into urban planning. Every day is a district. Every builder is a resident with a say in what a district becomes, because a verdict counts people, not plots: a whale with ten plots still counts once, and a newcomer with one free plot counts exactly as much.

A result is a skyline anyone can read. Quiet days stay Vacant. Solid days stand as Block. Days when a crowd shows up rise as Metropolis, towers lit on a city plate on a homepage, drawn live from chain data. And once in about thirty-three plots, a seed erupts: a Nova plot, a golden tower over its district, worth more than any verdict.

Why one district a day

A daily period keeps stakes small and habits strong. Missing a day costs one district, never a season. Sealing at 00:00 UTC and opening claims at 09:00 UTC gives every day two beats: a city freezes at midnight, and its builders collect in a morning, 16:00 in Jakarta, every day.

How Urbanova runs

  • Contracts on BNB Chain hold every rule: periods, prices, verdicts, claim amounts, fees. This website only reads them.
  • Building and claiming happen through a visitor's own Binance agent, guided by a public skill file. No wallet connects to this site.
  • Payments forward to a project wallet inside a same transaction that creates value. Contracts keep no spendable balance beyond a claim pool of $URBN.

Questions of fact are answered on chain. Start with a docs, a whitepaper, or a live board on a homepage.
