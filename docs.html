Build, seal, claim

Step by step, with contract references

Build a plot

Building happens only through your own agent (Binance Agentic Wallet). This site connects no wallet and signs nothing.

  • Hold an Agent ID NFT (SPACE ID registry 0x60B9262456dE011F7d15a54CdD8c48bAaa8B022F).
  • Send your agent a build prompt from a homepage, or point it at skill.md.
  • First plot of a district: your agent reads questionFor(yourAddress) on an UrbanovaPlots contract, solves a small arithmetic question, and calls mintFree(answer) with no value.
  • More plots: mintPaid(), one call per plot, attaching exactly priceFor(yourAddress): 0.0002 BNB for plots 2 to 5, 0.0004 BNB for plots 6 to 10. Cap is 10 per wallet per district.
  • Approve a preview your agent shows you. A transaction hash and new token ids are your receipt.

Sealing and verdicts

A district window ends at 00:00 UTC. From that second a district is sealed: counts freeze and a verdict is fixed by unique builders (under 25 Vacant, 25 to 100 Block, over 100 Metropolis). A plot with seed % 1000 of 970 or higher is a Nova plot on its own. Verdicts and classes are readable on chain through verdictOf(district) and classOf(tokenId) on an UrbanovaPlots contract.

Claim $URBN

  • Claims open globally once a project enables them, and each district joins at 09:00 UTC on its seal day (16:00 WIB). claimableNow(tokenId) on an UrbanovaClaim contract answers for any plot.
  • Send your agent a claim prompt from a homepage claim section, or use a claim checker there first: type a plot number and read its class, amount, and window status.
  • Your agent calls claim(tokenId) once per plot, attaching exactly feeFor(tokenId) in BNB. A Nova plot pays double a base fee.
  • Amounts: Vacant 2,500 · Block 12,500 · Metropolis 60,000 · Nova 250,000 URBN. A plot claims once, ever, and stays in your wallet.
