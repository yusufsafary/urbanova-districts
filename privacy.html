Privacy Policy

What this site does and does not collect

1 · Data this site collects

This is a static website. It runs no account system, sets no advertising cookies, and embeds no third-party trackers. A language choice (EN or zh-Hant) is stored in your own browser local storage so pages reopen in your language; it never leaves your device.

2 · On-chain data

Pages read public BNB Chain data through public RPC endpoints to display district counts, verdicts, and claim status. A claim checker sends a token id you type to those RPC endpoints as a read call. Wallet addresses and transaction data on a public blockchain are public by design and are not controlled by this site.

3 · Hosting logs

A hosting provider may keep standard server logs (IP address, user agent, requested page, time) for security and operations. Those logs are handled under a provider's own policy.

4 · What we never ask for

This site never asks for a seed phrase, a private key, or a password, and never will. Any page or message asking for one in Urbanova's name is fraudulent.

5 · Changes

This policy may be updated by publishing a revised version on this page.
